- credit / attribution (observability / tracing)
	- lineage of ideas
- certainty / confidence
- proof of work
	- do we go all crypto and start doing "proof of stake" to ensure the models are working for us and not frontier labs or themselves?
- formal verifiability / provable correctness
	- today models write scripts and run them; but against non-deterministic systems
	- if the systems were deterministic (nix) maybe we could save money by defining "proofs" in a more static fashion
		- i only think solving this is important if cost gets too high; the non-deterministic models are good enough at evaluating the non-deterministic systems
