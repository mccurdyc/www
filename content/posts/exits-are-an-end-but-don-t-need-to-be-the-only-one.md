If you want full control of your day, you must first achieve an exit that enables you to do so.

You _may_ be able to achieve similar, but not quite the same, control over your day-to-day by living well below your means. The challenge is that this ends up being quite a bit harder than it sounds. It's quite difficult to not allow "comforts" creep into your life when you can technically afford it; but these are traps. These are precisely the "golden handcuffs". Additionally, it's hard to leave money on the table when you know it's available to you.

- do you do your own lawn maintenance or do you pay someone?
- do you buy a higher quality car or do you deal with the maintenance burdens of something cheap?
- do you buy branded clothes that gain you respect on first impressions?
- do you live somewhere "fun", but that's also expensive because everyone wants to live somewhere "fun" or beautiful?
- do you travel?

Again, it's a balance. But if you can keep that pendulum more on the "below your means side" I think you gain some of that control that you are looking for out of an exit. Just a tiny smidge.
